Black Window

You searched for "Black Window", but Google showed you "Black Widow" (the movie)?

This website fixes that. It is a simple tool to create a full black window on your browser.

Uses for a Black Window:

  • Test monitor for dead pixels.
  • Check for backlight bleeding on LCD screens.
  • Pure black background for OLED energy saving.
  • Screen flashlight (White mode).
  • Focus and productivity background.

Click the button to open the palette and choose other colors or custom hex codes.
